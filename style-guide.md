# Sammii Spellbound — Character Bible & Style Guide

## The Character

**Sammii Spellbound** is the illustrated persona and brand mascot for a witchcraft, tarot, and astrology Instagram account. She serves as the visual anchor for all content — appearing in spell guides, tarot card illustrations, moon ritual posts, crystal guides, and astrology breakdowns. She is the personality layer that funnels audiences from Instagram to the Lunary app.

Sammii is confident, knowing, and quietly powerful. She has the energy of someone who reads your tarot and already knows what the cards will say. She is elegant without being untouchable — approachable but mysterious.

---

## Face & Features

Based on the original sketch (IMG_9566 — the red pencil portrait with updo and structured robe).

- **Eyes:** Large and expressive with detailed irises, visible lashes. Slightly narrowed — a knowing, composed gaze. Not wide-eyed innocent; she looks like she has secrets.
- **Brows:** Defined, slightly arched. Confident.
- **Nose:** Small, delicate, minimal detail — suggested rather than fully rendered.
- **Lips:** Full, soft, slightly parted or with a subtle closed-mouth expression. Never a big grin — she smirks, she gazes, she contemplates.
- **Face shape:** Slightly elongated, elegant. Strong jawline tapering to a defined chin.
- **Ears:** Visible when hair is up — can feature earrings (crescent moons, stars, crystals).

## Hair

Sammii has multiple looks (inspired by Margaery Tyrell's wardrobe of hairstyles across Game of Thrones):

### Primary Looks

1. **The Updo** (signature/default) — Hair swept up into a loose, textured bun with face-framing strands falling down. Elegant, witchy priestess energy. _Reference: IMG_9566._
2. **Half-Up Flowing** — Top section pulled back, rest flowing down in soft waves. Good for more casual/approachable content. _Reference: Margaery's earlier seasons look._
3. **Loose & Wavy** — Hair fully down, long flowing waves. Mystical, free-spirited energy. Good for moon/nature-themed content.
4. **Braided Crown** — Intricate braids woven around the head like a crown, with tendrils loose. Regal, ritual energy. _Reference: Margaery's wedding/court hairstyles._

### Hair Colour

Warm brunette with subtle highlights that catch light — not black, not blonde. Can have faint warm tones (auburn, honey) depending on the scene lighting. Avoid pure black or stark platinum.

## Wardrobe

Inspired by Margaery Tyrell's costuming — structured but flowing, powerful but feminine. Adapted for a modern witchy/spiritual context.

### Key Pieces

1. **The Structured Robe/Wrap** (signature) — Open collar, architectural shoulders, flowing sleeves. The outfit from the original sketch. Deep jewel tones. This is her "casting spells" or "reading tarot" outfit.
2. **Flowing Gown** — Floor-length, draped fabric, possibly one-shoulder or deep-V. For celestial/moon content. Lighter, more ethereal colours.
3. **High-Collar Dress** — More structured, fitted bodice with a high or mandarin collar. For informational/authoritative content (spell instructions, grimoire posts).
4. **Casual Wrap Top** — Simpler, more modern. For story content, Q&A posts, more personal/approachable moments.
5. **Ritual Cloak** — Hooded, dramatic, flowing. For Sabbat/seasonal content, shadow work, darker themes.

### Wardrobe Details

- Fabrics suggest texture: flowing silk, structured brocade, soft knits
- Necklines tend toward V-shapes, wraps, or open collars — never crew neck or casual
- Sleeves are often dramatic: bell sleeves, flowing, or structured shoulders
- Can incorporate subtle embroidery or celestial motifs on fabric

Softer and more romantic than Lunary's app palette, giving Sammii her own identity.

### Secondary/Seasonal Accents

- **Deep Teal** #2E6B6B — Water element, winter content
- **Burnt Amber** #C87941 — Autumn/Samhain content, fire element
- **Pale Blue** #B5CCE0 — Air element, clarity, new moon
- **Soft Black** #2D2A32 — Not pure black; used for linework and darkest shadows

### Palette Rules

- Backgrounds are predominantly **Warm Cream** or **Lavender** (light)
- **Midnight Plum** is for line work, shadows, and text — never large flat areas
- **Soft Gold** is used sparingly for magical accents — stars, sparkles, the moon necklace, glowing elements
- Each post should use 3-4 colours maximum from the palette
- The overall feel should be warm, soft, and inviting — not dark or gothic

### Relationship to Lunary

Lunary's app uses deeper purples and darker backgrounds. Sammii Spellbound's palette is the _daytime_ version of that world — lighter, warmer, more Instagram-friendly. The crescent moon necklace and lavender/plum tones are the visual bridge between the two brands.

## Art Style

Based on Sammii's original hand-drawn sketches. The style sits between semi-realistic and fashion illustration, with subtle anime/manga influence in the eyes.

### Line Work

- Confident, flowing lines — not rigid or overly clean
- Varies between thicker contour lines and thinner detail lines
- Hair is rendered with long, sweeping strokes — individual strands suggested, not drawn one by one
- Sketchy energy is part of the charm — not hyper-polished

### Colouring Style

- **Soft watercolour / digital watercolour feel** — colours bleed gently, not flat or cell-shaded
- Subtle gradients within skin tones
- Highlights left as negative space (the cream/white of the background showing through)
- Shadows in plum/lavender tones rather than grey or black
- Gold accents can have a slight glow or shimmer effect

### Proportions

- Slightly stylised: larger eyes, longer neck, more elegant proportions than photorealistic
- Hands are simplified but expressive (holding cards, crystals, grimoire)
- Body proportions lean toward fashion illustration — elongated, graceful

### What This Is NOT

- Not chibi/kawaii
- Not full anime
- Not photorealistic
- Not flat vector illustration
- Not dark/gothic aesthetic

## Content Types & Poses

### Tarot Card Posts

Sammii appears AS the character on the card. Different outfit and pose for each card. Background elements relate to the card's meaning. Card border/frame in soft gold. Card name in elegant serif typography.

### Spell/Ritual Guides

Sammii shown performing the spell — lighting candles, arranging crystals, holding herbs. Carousel format with step-by-step illustrations. Text overlaid in the Midnight Plum colour.

### Daily Horoscope / Astrology

Sammii in a contemplative pose with the relevant zodiac constellation or symbol incorporated. Could be holding a crystal ball, gazing at stars, or reading from her grimoire.

### Moon Phase Content

Different versions of Sammii for different phases — energetic and bright for full moon, introspective and shadowed for new moon. The crescent necklace matches the current phase.

### Crystal / Herb Guides

Close-up still life illustrations in the same style, with Sammii's hand holding or presenting the crystal/herb. Maintains the character connection without needing a full figure.

### Quote / Affirmation Posts

Text-focused with decorative celestial borders, small Sammii vignette in corner, or just her hand holding a relevant object.

## Typography

### Primary Font

An elegant serif font for headings and card names — something like Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or similar.

### Secondary Font

A clean, readable sans-serif for body text and longer spell instructions.

### Accent Font

A script/calligraphy font used sparingly for magical words, spell names, or "Sammii Spellbound" wordmark.

### Text Colour

Primarily **Midnight Plum** (#4A3352) on light backgrounds. **Warm Cream** (#F5EDE3) on dark backgrounds. Never pure black or pure white text.

## LoRA Training Notes

For training an AI model to reproduce this style consistently:

### Training Images Needed

- 15-20 cleaned-up sketches in a consistent style
- Ideally a mix of: face close-ups, bust/portrait, full body, hands/detail shots
- Both the red pencil and graphite sketches can be used
- If possible, 3-5 coloured reference pieces to establish the colour style

### Key Style Tokens to Capture

- The specific eye style (large, detailed iris, visible lashes, slightly narrowed)
- The line weight variation (thick contours, thin details)
- The hair rendering approach (flowing strokes)
- The slightly elongated elegant proportions
- The overall "sketchy but confident" quality

### Recommended Approach

1. Clean up best sketches digitally (increase contrast, white background)
2. Create 3-5 coloured versions manually or with AI assistance to establish colour reference
3. Train a SDXL or Flux LoRA with mixed sketch + coloured examples
4. Use trigger word like "sammii_style" or "spellbound_style"
5. Test with prompts describing various Sammii poses and outfits

### Consistency Checks

When generating new content, always verify:

- Crescent moon necklace is present
- Eyes match the signature style
- Colour palette stays within the defined range
- Line quality feels hand-drawn, not airbrushed or photorealistic

## Brand Voice (for captions)

Sammii Spellbound speaks in a warm, knowledgeable tone. She's the friend who happens to know a lot about witchcraft and astrology. Not preachy, not gatekeeping, not overly mystical-sounding.

- Uses "you" directly — speaks to the reader
- Practical and grounded — "here's how to actually do this"
- Occasionally playful — light humour, not taking herself too seriously
- Never condescending about beginner questions
- Weaves in astrological/seasonal context naturally
